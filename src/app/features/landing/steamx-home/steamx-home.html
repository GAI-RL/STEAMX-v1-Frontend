What is STEAMX and how does it work?

+

STEAMX is an AI-powered education platform for Grade 9–12 students in Pakistan. Students choose their board, grade, and subject, and the platform instantly generates board-style tests. After each test, detailed analytics identify weak areas and the AI tutor provides targeted recommendations.

Help Center Contact Us Community

Is STEAMX aligned with Pakistani Board curricula?

+

Yes! STEAMX is fully aligned with all major Pakistani boards including Punjab Board, Sindh Board, KPK Board, and Federal Board. Our content is regularly updated to match the latest curriculum changes.

Do I need any technical skills to use STEAMX?

+

Not at all! STEAMX is designed to be simple and intuitive. Just sign up, select your subjects, and start learning. No technical expertise required.

How is STEAMX different from a traditional coaching center?

+

STEAMX offers 24/7 access, personalized AI-powered learning, instant feedback, and costs a fraction of traditional coaching centers. Learn anytime, anywhere at your own pace.

Is student data safe on STEAMX?

+

Absolutely! We take data security seriously. STEAMX is fully compliant with FERPA and COPPA regulations. Your data is encrypted and never shared with third parties.
